Vrelly Privacy Policy

App: Vrelly (package com.goalwise.app) · Developer: Winix Studio · Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Vrelly app ("Vrelly", "the app", "we") handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Vrelly is a personal expense tracking and financial goals app.

1. Summary

  • Vrelly has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • The expenses, budgets, goals and other financial entries you add are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to any server, and we cannot see them.
  • The AI money coach (spending analysis, subscription suggestions and goal planning) runs on your device. Your financial data is not sent to us or to any third party for this feature.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain device and usage data, described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in Vrelly (transactions, categories, amounts, notes, budgets and goals) is saved in the app's local storage on your phone. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted by the app. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted from your device. We do not have a copy, so we cannot restore it.

3. Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, Vrelly uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). The SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google:

Data typeExamplesPurposes
Device or other identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set IDAdvertising or marketing, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance
Approximate locationDerived from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
App interactionsAd views and taps, app launches
DiagnosticsInformation about ad and SDK performance, such as crashes or errors

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

4. Information we do not collect

Vrelly does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or bank account credentials. The app does not connect to your bank, and it does not use analytics or crash reporting services of its own.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google as described in section 3, the app does not share information with third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your financial data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google according to its own policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

7. Children's privacy

Vrelly is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, please contact us.

8. Security

Your financial data never leaves your device through the app. Data sent by the advertising SDK is protected in transit using TLS encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your device with a screen lock.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if new features change how data is handled. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
